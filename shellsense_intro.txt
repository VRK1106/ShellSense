🚀 Hey everyone! 

Tired of having 50 browser tabs open just to do quick math, convert time zones, search for your latest download, or set a simple reminder? 

I’ve been working on a lightweight, keyboard-first desktop utility called ShellSense. It’s a clean, frosted-glass command palette (think Spotlight or Raycast for Windows) that lets you run instant utilities entirely through natural language. Just press Ctrl + Shift + Space and start typing. 

Here is a quick breakdown of what it solves and how to use it:

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
💡 What ShellSense Does (Problems & Solutions)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Instant Calculations & Unit Conversions
   * The Problem: Opening calculator apps or googling "15% of 80" or "10 miles to km" breaks your focus.
   * The Solution: Do it right from your keyboard.
     * Try: 15% of 80 or sqrt(144) or 10 miles to km

2. Timezone Translation
   * The Problem: Squinting at calendar invites trying to figure out what 10:30 AM EST is in IST.
   * The Solution: Instant conversion.
     * Try: 10:30 am est to ist

3. No-Click Currency Exchange
   * The Problem: Checking exchange rates requires googling, which loads heavy webpages.
   * The Solution: Quick, real-time-like currency conversions.
     * Try: 100 usd to eur

4. The "Where is my file?" Finder
   * The Problem: You just downloaded a file or PDF, but your download folder is a mess and you have to open File Explorer to dig it up.
   * The Solution: Open or copy your most recent download instantly.
     * Try: open last download or copy last download

5. Quick Snippets & File Shortcuts
   * The Problem: Copying your email address, standard git messages, or finding specific deep project folders over and over again.
   * The Solution: Bind them to shorthand keys to open or copy them instantly.
     * Try: copy email or open [your-shorthand-key]

6. Distraction-Free Alarms & Timers
   * The Problem: Setting a timer on your phone distracts you, and browser-based timers are annoying.
   * The Solution: Set background alarms that trigger native Windows alerts.
     * Try: timer 5 mins to check oven or stop timer

7. System Cleanup & Tune-up
   * The Problem: Windows boot times get slow because of hidden startup apps, or temp folders are eating up SSD space.
   * The Solution: Audit startup apps and clear caches with a single command.
     * Try: show startup apps, disable Spotify from startup, or clean temp files

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
📦 How to get it running:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Clone / Download the repository to your local machine.
2. Run "pip install -r requirements.txt" to grab the lightweight dependencies.
3. Double-click "run.bat" to launch it silently in the background (it sits neatly in your system tray).
4. Press "Ctrl + Shift + Space" to summon the frosted-glass bar, type "?" or "help" to see the full list, and enjoy!
