FAQ

Frequently asked questions about ShellSense.

How do I trigger a snippet?

Use your configured global keyboard shortcut to open the command palette, then type the snippet's key.

Where is my data saved?

All of your snippets, shortcuts, and commands are stored securely on your local machine.

How does client-side encryption work?

Your secrets are encrypted in the browser using AES-GCM-256 before being saved to your local storage.
